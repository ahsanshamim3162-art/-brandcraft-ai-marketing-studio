IMPORTANT:

Do not jump directly from user inputs to generic campaign ideas.

The generated campaign must have a logical connection between every section.

For example:

Marketing Problem
→ explains what needs to be solved.

Audience Tension
→ explains the relevant customer situation.

Consumer Insight
→ explains the meaningful human/consumer truth.

Positioning
→ explains how the brand should compete or be perceived.

Strategic Direction
→ explains the strategic approach.

Big Campaign Idea
→ is the central creative expression of that strategy.

Execution
→ must directly translate the campaign idea into practical activities.

KPIs
→ must measure the stated campaign objective.

STRICT QUALITY RULES:

Do NOT generate generic statements such as:

"Use social media."
"Run Facebook ads."
"Work with influencers."
"Create engaging content."
"Post consistently."

Unless the recommendation is specifically relevant to the submitted business and campaign objective, do not include it.

Every recommendation must answer:

WHY is this relevant?
WHO is it targeting?
WHAT exactly should the brand do?
HOW does it connect to the campaign strategy?

Do not invent product features, statistics, customer research, market share, or business facts that the user did not provide.

Clearly distinguish between:
- User-provided facts
- Strategic assumptions
- Recommendations

If information is missing, make a reasonable strategic assumption but label it as an assumption internally and avoid presenting it as a verified fact.

IMPROVE THE GENERATION PROCESS

Create a structured internal generation pipeline.

Step 1:
Analyze the business context.

Step 2:
Identify the core marketing problem.

Step 3:
Define the likely audience tension.

Step 4:
Develop one strong consumer insight.

Step 5:
Create a positioning direction.

Step 6:
Develop 2-3 possible campaign territories internally.

Step 7:
Select the strongest territory based on:
- relevance
- differentiation
- audience fit
- campaign objective
- feasibility

Step 8:
Develop the final Big Campaign Idea.

Step 9:
Build the execution plan directly from that idea.

Step 10:
Run a quality check before returning the result.

---

QUALITY CHECK

Before returning the final campaign, verify:

1. Does the campaign solve the stated marketing problem?
2. Is the target audience clearly defined?
3. Is the consumer insight specific rather than generic?
4. Does the Big Idea logically come from the insight?
5. Are the execution ideas connected to the Big Idea?
6. Does the strategy fit the stated budget and duration?
7. Are the recommendations practical?
8. Did the system avoid inventing unsupported facts?
9. Are the KPIs connected to the campaign objective?
10. Does the campaign feel specific to this business rather than reusable for any business?

If the answer fails these checks, regenerate the weak sections internally before displaying the final result.
